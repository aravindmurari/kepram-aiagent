# Kepram LLC — Company Overview

Kepram is AI infrastructure for small and mid-size business. Tagline: **"AI that works."** Based in Atlanta, GA.

Kepram builds **two things**, both trained on the client's own business and live in days, not months:

1. **AI Front Desk** — a turnkey AI front desk that answers the client's calls, texts, and web chats.
2. **AI Custom Agents** — bespoke AI agents scoped to whatever specific problem the client has.

Everything Kepram builds is trained on the client's real business — their products, pricing, policies, and process — not a generic template and not an auto-scrape of their website.

> Note on the two products: AI Front Desk is the packaged, turnkey offer. AI Custom Agents is built to order. The live chat assistant a visitor is talking to on the website is itself an example of an AI Custom Agent — a real, working preview trained on Kepram's own business.

## Why Kepram — the difference is what's under the hood

**1. A complete behavioral blueprint.**
Off-the-shelf tools (Tidio, Intercom, Gorgias, and similar) give you 5–10 instruction slots to configure a bot. Kepram writes directly to the model — no slot limits. Conversation flow, escalation logic, upsell triggers, and product rules all run at once.

**2. Designed to close, not deflect.**
Template chat tools are built to keep support tickets out of your inbox. Kepram agents are built to move prospects through a buying journey: listen, educate, recommend, commit.

**3. Flat cost. No surprise invoices.**
Per-conversation billing spikes unpredictably — a campaign that drives 10× the traffic shouldn't 10× your bill. Kepram's cost stays flat regardless of volume.

**4. No lock-in. No surprises.**
Flat monthly cost, no long-term contracts. Pause or stop anytime. Kepram earns the business every month. If something isn't working, the client says so and Kepram fixes it — or they walk away.

## How it works — from conversation to live agent in days

Each step has a clear deliverable. No long discovery, no black boxes, no lock-in.

1. **Understand** — One 30-minute call. Kepram learns the business: products, customers, and the questions the team answers over and over. No intake forms, no jargon.
2. **Build** — A working demo, trained on the client. The client talks to it, breaks it, says what's wrong. Kepram rebuilds and re-trains until it sounds like someone on their team.
3. **Live** — Deployed on the client's own domain, with the lead handoff wired to their team and a walkthrough of the conversation log. The team uses it the same day.
4. **Ongoing** — It just runs. Flat monthly cost, plus a monthly dashboard showing what it's doing (bookings, contacts, and directions delivered). Kepram refreshes the knowledge as the business changes. Pause or stop anytime.
