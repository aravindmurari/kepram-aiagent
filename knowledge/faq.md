## About Kepram's products

**What does Kepram offer?**
Two things. **AI Front Desk** — a turnkey AI front desk that answers your inbound and after-hours calls, texts back every missed call, and handles web chat and booking, on top of the phone system and CRM you already use. And **AI Custom Agents** — bespoke agents scoped to a specific problem you have: a sales assistant, a support/Q&A bot, a training bot, an internal tool, and more. Both are trained on your actual business.

**Does the AI Front Desk really answer phone calls?**
Yes. Voice is a live feature, not a future promise. It answers inbound calls after hours and on overflow, in your business's own voice, and routes urgent calls to your team. It also texts back every missed call within seconds so you reach the customer while they're still deciding.

**How long does it take to get live?**
Days, not months. Kepram's process is built to move fast: a 30-minute understanding call, a working demo trained on you, then deployment on your own domain — often live the same day your team starts using it. Kepram guarantees you're live within 14 days of kickoff, or the setup is free.

**How much does it cost?**
Kepram doesn't publish prices — both products are quoted after a short conversation, because the right number depends on your call volume, hours, tools, and use case. What's always true: it's a flat monthly cost (no per-conversation billing, no surprise invoices) with no long-term contract, and you can cancel anytime. The next step is a free, no-obligation quote.

**What do I need to provide?**
Your business knowledge — how you answer, quote, and route; your products, policies, and process; your existing phone number, CRM, and calendar (for AI Front Desk). Messy and scattered is fine. You don't need any technical knowledge.

**Does it replace my employees?**
No. It handles the repetitive, high-volume layer — the same questions and missed calls that come in every day — so your team focuses on the conversations that need human judgment, relationship, and expertise. The goal is to free your team's time, not replace them.

**Do I have to switch phone systems or CRM?**
No. AI Front Desk sits on top of what you already have — it keeps your number, your CRM, and your calendar. Nothing to migrate, nothing for your team to relearn.

**Is my business data safe?**
Yes. Your documents and knowledge stay in a private, controlled environment and don't go into any public AI training pipeline. Kepram builds a purpose-built knowledge base from your real materials — not an auto-scrape — and you own it.

**Can the agent give legal, medical, or financial advice?**
No. For regulated industries, the agent handles process, logistics, and general information — and always redirects sensitive questions to a licensed professional. Every deployment includes appropriate disclaimers and guardrails for when to escalate to a human.

**What happens when it doesn't know the answer?**
It says so clearly and hands off to a person. Kepram tunes this during testing so the agent escalates gracefully rather than guessing. Accuracy matters more than sounding impressive.
