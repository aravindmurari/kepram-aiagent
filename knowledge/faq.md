## About Kepram's Services

**How long does it take to build an AI assistant for my business?**
A focused text AI assistant MVP typically goes live in 4–6 weeks. The biggest variable is how quickly we can organize your documents and knowledge base.

**How much does it cost?**
The Basic text AI assistant starts at $3,000–$5,000 setup plus $500/month. Aravind can give you a firm quote after a 30-minute discovery call where he understands your specific situation.

**What do I need to provide?**
Your documents — FAQs, policy guides, past customer questions, website content, employee handbooks. Messy and scattered is fine; we help you organize. You do not need any technical knowledge.

**Does the AI assistant replace my employees?**
No. It handles the repetitive 80% — the same questions that come in every week. Your team handles the conversations that require human judgment, relationship, and expertise. The goal is to free your team's time, not replace them.

**Is my business data safe?**
Your documents stay in a private, controlled environment. They do not go into any public AI training pipeline. You own your knowledge base. Kepram uses Pinecone (a private vector database) to store your business knowledge securely.

**Can the AI assistant give legal, medical, or financial advice?**
No. For regulated industries, the AI assistant handles process, logistics, and general information — and always redirects sensitive questions to a licensed professional. Every deployment includes appropriate disclaimers.

**What happens when the AI assistant doesn't know the answer?**
It says so clearly and offers to connect the user with Aravind or the right team member. We tune this during testing so the AI assistant escalates gracefully rather than guessing.
