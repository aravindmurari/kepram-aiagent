# AI Custom Agents — Kepram's Built-to-Order Product

**One line:** One engine, any problem. Kepram builds a custom AI agent scoped to the client's specific problem — the same core engine, with clear goals, the client's knowledge files, and guardrails, pointed at whatever they need.

This is Kepram's **bespoke** product. Where AI Front Desk is turnkey, Custom Agents are scoped per use case. The live chat assistant on Kepram's own website is an example of an AI Custom Agent.

## What it can be

If it involves answering questions, moving a conversation forward, or working from the client's knowledge, Kepram can build an agent for it:

- **Sales assistant** — Qualifies leads, answers product and pricing questions, and moves buyers toward a booking or a handoff to the team. Listen, educate, recommend, close. Includes lead capture and booking.
- **Support & Q&A bot** — Answers customer and staff questions straight from the client's policies, docs, and product info, and hands off to a person when it should. Answers from the knowledge base; escalates when appropriate.
- **Training bot** — Onboards and coaches the client's team on process, product, and policy, available the moment someone has a question. Consistent, on-brand answers, always on for new hires.
- **Internal tools** — Automates repetitive internal work: lookups, summaries, drafting, and pulling answers out of the documents a team searches all day. Scoped to the workflow, private to the team.

...and more — these are examples, not a fixed menu.

## The engine — same three parts, every time

Every agent is built from the same core engine. What changes is what Kepram points it at:

1. **Goals — what it's for.** Kepram defines exactly what the agent does and what a win looks like, so it stays pointed at the client's outcome instead of wandering.
2. **Knowledge — what it knows.** Kepram loads the client's products, policies, and process as its knowledge base. Not a scrape of the website — the real thing.
3. **Guardrails — what it won't do.** Kepram sets the rules for what it says, when to escalate to a human, and where to stop. Confident inside the lines, never past them.
