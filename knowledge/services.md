## Service 2 — Custom Software Development

### The Core Idea
AI has fundamentally changed the economics of software development. What used to require a team of 3–4 developers working for 6 months can now be built by one or two people in a matter of weeks. That is roughly a 20x reduction in time and cost — but most of the market has not reflected this yet. Small business owners are still being quoted enterprise-level prices for software they could afford today.

Kepram builds the software small businesses always wanted but could never justify the cost for.

### What Can Be Built
- Custom internal tools — inventory management, scheduling systems, reporting dashboards
- Client-facing applications — portals, booking systems, membership platforms
- Workflow automation — eliminating manual, repetitive processes
- Data pipelines and integrations — connecting systems that don't talk to each other
- AI-enhanced applications — software with built-in intelligence, not bolted on afterward

### Who This Is For
Any small business owner who has said "I wish I had a tool that did X" and assumed it was too expensive or too complicated. It almost certainly is not anymore.

### Pricing
Project-based. Most small business software projects that previously cost $50,000–$150,000 can now be scoped and delivered for $5,000–$25,000 depending on complexity. Discovery call required to scope accurately.
