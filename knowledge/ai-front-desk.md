# AI Front Desk — Kepram's Turnkey Product

**One line:** Never miss another call. AI Front Desk answers inbound calls after hours and on overflow, texts back every missed call, and handles web chat and booking — running on top of the phone system and CRM the client already uses, with no disruption.

This is Kepram's **packaged, turnkey** product. It's the same offer for every client (the knowledge is trained on their specific business), so it stands up fast.

## What it does — one front desk, every channel

Calls, texts, and chats are all handled by the same system, so nothing slips through after hours or during a rush.

- **After-hours & overflow calls** — When the office is closed or every line is busy, the call still gets answered. Callers get real answers in the business's own voice, not a voicemail box. Urgent calls route to the client's team.
- **Missed-call text-back** — Every missed call gets a text within seconds, so the lead hears from the business before they call the next name on the list. Automatic, instant follow-up that keeps the conversation going by SMS.
- **Web chat & SMS** — The same assistant answers on the website and over text, trained on the client's services, pricing, and policies. One assistant across channels that captures and qualifies leads.
- **Appointment booking** — Books calls and visits straight into the client's calendar during the conversation, confirming and reminding by text. No back-and-forth.

## Why it matters

- **Every call answered** — After hours, at lunch, during a rush, the phone still gets picked up. No caller hits a dead end. The call you miss is the job you lose.
- **Speed to lead** — A missed call gets a text back in seconds, reaching the customer while they're still deciding, not a day later.
- **No disruption** — It keeps the client's number, CRM, and calendar. Nothing to migrate, nothing for the team to relearn.

## Works with what you already have

AI Front Desk sits **on top of** the client's current setup — no rip and replace. Kepram wires it into existing tools so it feels like an extension of the team from day one:

- **Your phone number** — keeps the existing line; calls forward on no-answer, after hours, or on overflow.
- **Your CRM** — new leads and contacts flow into the system the team already works in.
- **Your calendar** — books straight into existing scheduling; no separate tool to check.
- **Your process** — trained on how the business actually answers, quotes, and routes, so it sounds like them.

## Who it's for

Any business that lives and dies by the phone and can't afford to miss calls: home services and trades, healthcare practices, retail, professional services, and any business taking inbound calls, especially after hours or at high volume.
